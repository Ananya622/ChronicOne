# ChronicOne 🩺

### A simple and personalized way to stay consistent with chronic condition care.

ChronicOne is a web application designed to help people manage their daily health routines and stay consistent with the habits that matter to them.

Instead of keeping health-related information scattered across notes or different apps, ChronicOne brings daily check-ins, habit adherence, health measurements, mood, progress, and recovery into one place.

The application adapts the tracking experience according to the user's selected health conditions.

---

## 🌱 Why ChronicOne?

Managing a chronic condition is not only about recording health measurements. Long-term consistency also matters.

ChronicOne focuses on questions like:

- How consistent have I been recently?
- Did I complete today's important habits?
- How is my adherence changing over time?
- What happened on a particular day?
- How are my health measurements changing?
- What happens if I miss a few days?
- Can I get back on track after a setback?

The goal is to make health tracking feel **simple, visual, and encouraging** rather than like a medical form.

---

## ✨ What Can You Do With ChronicOne?

### 📋 Daily Check-in

Users can complete their daily health-related habits and get an automatic adherence score.

The day is classified as:

- 🟢 Full
- 🔵 Partial
- 🟠 Low
- 🔴 Missed

The dashboard then gives a quick overview of the day's progress.

---

### 🔥 Streak & Consistency Tracking

ChronicOne keeps track of:

- Current streak
- Best streak
- Grace days
- Relapse detection
- Recovery progress
- Recovery bonus

The recovery system is designed to encourage users to get back on track instead of treating one setback as complete failure.

---

### 🛡️ Grace Days & Recovery

Users have a limited number of grace days that can protect their consistency during occasional missed days.

If a relapse occurs, ChronicOne can track a recovery period.

Completing the recovery period can provide an additional recovery bonus.

This creates a more realistic consistency system instead of simply resetting everything after one bad day.

---

### ❤️ Health & Biomarkers

The Health section allows users to record condition-related measurements such as:

- Blood Pressure
- Body Weight
- Heart Rate
- Blood Glucose
- Peak Flow
- Joint Pain
- Sleep Duration

Health records are stored with their dates so historical readings can be viewed separately.

The biomarkers shown can also change according to the user's selected health conditions.

---

### 😊 Wellness & Mood

Users can record how they are feeling physically and mentally.

This allows wellness and mood information to be viewed alongside daily adherence.

---

### 📅 Calendar & Adherence Heatmap

The Calendar provides a visual overview of consistency over time.

Each day is represented using a color based on its adherence level.

Users can:

- View a complete month
- Navigate between months
- See the last 7 days of consistency
- Hover over a day for a quick summary
- Click a day to see detailed information

The Day Details view can show:

- Overall adherence
- Habit-by-habit percentages
- Daily notes
- Mood
- Health measurements recorded on that date

---

### 📊 Dashboard

The dashboard brings the most important information together in one place.

It includes:

- Current streak
- Best streak
- Grace days
- Today's adherence
- Today's habit progress
- Wellness & mood
- Recent consistency
- Health biomarkers
- Achievement progress
